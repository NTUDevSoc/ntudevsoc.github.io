Hack A Duck 2026

Code. Create. Collaborate.

What is Hack A Duck?

Hack A Duck is NTU's annual hackathon, organised by the Department of Computer Science, with support from the Developer Society (DevSoc), the AI & Robotics Society, industry sponsors, staff, and student volunteers.

Taking place from Thursday 29 October to Friday 30 October 2026, Hack A Duck brings together students from across Computing for 24 hours of innovation, teamwork, learning, and fun. Whether you're an experienced developer or completely new to hackathons, this is your opportunity to build something exciting, meet new people, and develop your skills in a supportive environment.

Best of all, it's completely free to attend.

Event Details

Date & Start Time
29-30 October 2026

What You'll Do

Choose a Challenge
Teams can select from a range of challenge briefs provided by the Department of Computer Science and event sponsors. You'll spend the hackathon designing, developing and presenting a solution to a real-world problem.

Whether your interests lie in software development, AI, cybersecurity, web technologies, robotics, data science, or something completely different, there will be opportunities to create something exciting.
Learn Something New
Throughout the event there will be a variety of talks, workshops and drop-in sessions designed to help you:
  • Learn new technologies and tools
  • Develop your technical and professional skills
  • Gain insights from industry professionals
  • Find inspiration for your project
No matter your experience level, there will be opportunities to learn and explore new ideas.
Meet Other Students
Hack A Duck is one of the largest student events run by the Department of Computer Science and is a fantastic opportunity to connect with students across different years and courses.

Work with new teammates, build your network, and become part of the wider computing community at NTU.
Take a Break
Hackathons are about more than just coding. Throughout the event there will be opportunities to take a break from your project and enjoy:
  • Games and activities
  • Social events
  • Networking opportunities
  • Community challenges
The goal is to create a fun, welcoming atmosphere where everyone can enjoy the experience.
Food & Refreshments Included
Food and refreshments will be provided throughout the event.

A catering form will be circulated to registered participants closer to the event so that dietary requirements and preferences can be accommodated where possible.

Overnight Arrangements

Hack A Duck runs overnight, but participants are not expected to remain in the Computer Science building throughout the night.

Teams are encouraged to make use of the University's library study spaces and other campus learning spaces during overnight working hours.

The Computer Science building will close overnight and reopen from 9:00am on Friday 30 October, when participants can return to continue working on their projects and prepare for the final presentations.

Please ensure you work responsibly, take appropriate breaks, and look after your wellbeing throughout the event.


Who Can Attend?

Hack A Duck is open to:
  • Undergraduate students in Year 1
  • Undergraduate students in Year 2
  • Undergraduate students on Placement Year
  • Undergraduate students in Year 3
  • Degree Apprenticeship students
  • MSc students
MSc students are welcome to attend and participate but will be allocated to a group of undergraduate students to encourage collaboration across different levels of experience.

Registration

Register Your Place
Registration closes on 16 October 2026.

When registering, you'll be able to secure your place and choose a team name. If you do not already have a team, don't worry, we'll help you find one on the day.

A catering request form will be sent to registered participants approximately three weeks before the event.

  • Learn from workshops and industry talks
